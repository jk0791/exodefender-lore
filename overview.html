Overview

Humanity has unlocked interstellar travel and begun establishing colonies across distant worlds. Early expansion showed promise—until first contact with the C’tarr-ni.

An ancient warrior species, the C’tarr-ni believe they were granted absolute dominion over the galaxy. They tolerate no rivals. Earth’s first colonies were wiped out without warning.

For years, every new foothold was discovered and destroyed. Unable to defend against the C’tarr-ni, humanity was forced to retreat—pushing further into the outer reaches of Orion’s Arm.

A new strategy emerged.

Small ground units are now deployed to remote worlds, each tasked with establishing a foothold strong enough to survive long-term. If even one succeeds, it could shift the balance of the war.

But the C’tarr-ni are already hunting them.

Their patrols scour these systems, striking with overwhelming force. Against their weapons and shields, our ground units cannot survive alone.

They need air support.

Many systems were developed to fill this role. Only one proved effective. A fast, heavily armed space interdictor built for rapid response and precision strikes:

The Exo-57 Striker.
